Caret Launcher is a keyboard-first home screen: you open apps by typing.
Whenever you go home, the cursor is already in the search field and the keyboard is up.

I built it for myself and decided to share it. There are no plans to make money from it.
It's open source under the Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/malt03/caret-launcher

■ Type an app's name to open it
・The keyboard is always ready on the home screen.
・Search doesn't care about upper or lower case.
・Give apps names of your own: "gallery" or "fotos" for your photos app, the old name of an app that was renamed, or a short nickname.

■ Home screen
・When you haven't typed anything, the space above the keyboard is a home screen built on Android's own launcher.
・Swipe up for the full list of apps.
・Pick themed icons and an icon shape in Wallpaper & style.
・On a foldable, unfold it to get two panes: all your apps on the left and your home screen on the right.

■ Search straight from what you typed
・Choose up to three apps, and their buttons appear at the right end of the search field. A browser or an AI app works well.
・Type some text and tap a button to hand it to that app.

■ Privacy
・Caret Launcher has no permission to access the internet. What you type and which apps you open never leave your phone.
・No ads, no tracking, no sign-up.
